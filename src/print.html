How to save as PDF
  • Windows, Mac, Chromebook: Print, then choose Save as PDF (or Microsoft Print to PDF) as the printer.
  • Android: Print, then choose Save as PDF at the top.
  • iPhone, iPad: Print, then tap Share (or pinch out on the preview) and choose Save to Files.
  • For badge size, print or save at 100% scale (Actual size), not Fit to page.
Printed cards and saved PDFs are controlled items. Anyone holding one can authenticate. Don't email or share the PDF, and report a lost card to the custodian.
